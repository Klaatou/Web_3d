## Annonce longue, anglais

```
**🌍 Web 3D: your OpenSim region in a web page**

Got an OpenSim region? Export it as an OAR, convert it to GLB, and your visitors can walk around it straight from their browser: no viewer, no install.

**What it does**
• Third-person walking with collisions
• Live visitors and chat, in the same space
• Zones and portals to link several regions
• World map
• Tiled loading for very large regions
• Publishes on any web host (GitHub Pages works fine)

**Get started without reading all the docs**
Two ready-to-use zips: `region-simple.zip` (your GLB in a browser) and `galerie-photos.zip` (a 3D gallery of 30 photos: just swap the images). Unzip, double-click the launcher, then copy the `site/` folder to your host.

🎮 Try the live demo (3 regions, portals, live presence and chat): https://klaatou.github.io/Web_3d_demo/
📦 Downloads: https://github.com/Klaatou/Web_3d/releases/latest
💻 Code (MIT license): https://github.com/Klaatou/Web_3d

Feedback and questions welcome! 🙂
```
